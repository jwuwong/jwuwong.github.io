Outreach

I am committed to the social wellness of immigrant communities in the United States. My research is grounded in and gives back to these communities, and I actively seek collaborations with Chinese immigrant organizations, community advocates, educators, students, and anyone whose work touches the lives of immigrants and multilingual communities.

If you are part of a community organization, a fellow researcher, a student, or simply someone interested in language, immigration, and community wellbeing, I would love to hear from you. Whether it's a potential collaboration, a community project, or a conversation, please reach out.
