Projects

I am a linguist that uses qualitative and quantative methods to study how people produce, perceive, and evaluate speech.

Regional Perception of Immigrant Talkers

Built and ran listening experiments measuring how listeners perceive and categorize talkers by accent and origin, analyzing behavioral response data with mixed-effects models to isolate systematic perceptual patterns from individual rater variation.
Methods: perception experiments, mixed-effects modeling · Python, R

Acoustic Variation in Immigrant Speech Communities

Collected, segmented, and annotated audio data from Cantonese immigrant speakers, quantifying fine-grained acoustic-phonetic variation and its social correlates through large-scale acoustic measurement and annotation workflows.
Methods: acoustic analysis, audio annotation, quantitative sociophonetics · Praat, ELAN, R

Intensifier Variation and Change

Co-authored a corpus-based study of intensifier variation in Salinas, California, drawing on sociolinguistic interview data and using quantitative variationist methods and mixed-effects models to model language change across speakers. Published in Penn Working Papers in Linguistics.
Methods: corpus analysis, mixed-effects modeling, variationist statistics · R
