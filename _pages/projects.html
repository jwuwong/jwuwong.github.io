Projects

Perception of Category-Divergent Talkers

Designed and ran forced-choice (2AFC) perception experiments measuring how listeners categorize ambiguous and category-divergent speech—methodologically parallel to human preference elicitation and evaluation pipelines used in ML.
Methods: experimental design, 2AFC tasks, statistical modeling · Python, R, Praat

Regional Perceptions of Immigrant Talkers

Ran listening experiments measuring how listeners perceive and categorize immigrant talkers, analyzing behavioral response data with mixed-effects models to identify systematic perceptual patterns.
Methods: perception experiments, mixed-effects modeling · Python, R

Vocalic Variation in Immigrant Communities

Collected and analyzed acoustic-phonetic data from Cantonese immigrant speakers in Boston, quantifying vocalic variation and its social correlates through large-scale acoustic measurement and annotation.
Methods: acoustic analysis, data annotation, quantitative sociophonetics · Praat, ELAN, R

Intensifier Variation and Change

Co-authored a corpus-based study of intensifier variation in Salinas, California, drawing on sociolinguistic interviews and using quantitative variationist methods and mixed-effects models to analyze language change across speakers. Published in Penn Working Papers in Linguistics.
Methods: corpus analysis, mixed-effects modeling, variationist statistics · R
