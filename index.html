Sociolinguistics · Language Contact · Immigration

I am a PhD candidate in Linguistics at Stanford University, currently working on my dissertation. My research is broadly concerned with the social life of language—particularly with what situations of contact between heterogeneous populations can reveal about the underlying mechanisms that animate our social, cultural, and linguistic worlds. I draw on a wide range of methodologies—survey, computational, and ethnographic—to answer these questions, and I focus on the levels of sound (phonetics and phonology) and meaning (semantics and pragmatics), with a particular interest in Chinese immigrant communities.
